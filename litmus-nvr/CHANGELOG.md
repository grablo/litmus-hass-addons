# Changelog

## 2.4.0

- Home Assistant integration: cameras, detection sensors, alerts and recordings in Home Assistant. Turn it on in Litmus › Settings › Integrations, and Litmus appears under Settings › Devices & services › Discovered.
- Apple Home: add each camera to the Home app with its code.
- Two-way audio on cameras that support it.
- Open Litmus in the Home Assistant sidebar without signing in again.
- Watchdog: turn it on to restart the add-on automatically if it stops responding.

## 2.3.0

- Automatic camera discovery: Add camera lists the cameras found on your network, checks the connection and shows a picture and the resolution.
- Low-resolution video for AI detection to reduce the load on your device. Recordings stay at the original resolution.
- Reconnects when a camera's IP address changes on the same network.
- Improved camera edit screen.
- Fixes: sound alerts include a snapshot; alert snapshots no longer fail while a camera is briefly disconnected.

## 2.2.1

- First release of Litmus NVR.
