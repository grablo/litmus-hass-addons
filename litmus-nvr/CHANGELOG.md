# Changelog

## 2.3.0

- Automatic camera discovery: Add camera lists the cameras found on your network, checks the connection and shows a picture and the resolution.
- Low-resolution video for AI detection to reduce the load on your device. Recordings stay at the original resolution.
- Reconnects when a camera's IP address changes on the same network.
- Improved camera edit screen.
- Fixes: sound alerts include a snapshot; alert snapshots no longer fail while a camera is briefly disconnected.
